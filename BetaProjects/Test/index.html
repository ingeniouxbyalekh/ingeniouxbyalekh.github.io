Who's behind this

I'm Alekh Chandra Sahoo, from Puri, Odisha, India, currently pursuing an MTech in Computer Science and Engineering at Odisha University of Technology and Research, following a BTech in Computer Science and Engineering from the Government College of Engineering, Kalahandi (GCEK), Bhawanipatna.

I like building software that actually solves a problem instead of just demonstrating one. Most of my time goes into understanding and building AI/ML models, and into research around cloud computing and data structures & algorithms — how systems store, move and reason over data at scale, and how the underlying algorithms make that efficient or not.
