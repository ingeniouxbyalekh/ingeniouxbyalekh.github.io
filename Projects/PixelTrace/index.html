How it works, and what it cannot do

Method

The image is cut into 8 × 8 pixel blocks. Each block stores one bit in the brightness pattern of its two lowest-frequency textures. A 66-byte frame (note, time and a CRC-32 checksum) is spread over a 24 × 22 block tile, and that tile repeats across the picture. A crop still holds every bit, and the reader votes across all the repeats before checking the checksum.

Limits

Rotation, perspective and photographing a screen will usually destroy the watermark. The payload is not encrypted or signed: anyone can extract it with this page, and anyone can overwrite it by re-embedding. Treat it as a marker, not as proof.
