How it works, and what it cannot do

Method

One short text is hidden four ways: a Fourier sync template (32 faint sinusoids) is added first so screenshots and resized copies can be rescaled exactly before reading, then Quaternion Zernike moments: the colour image becomes a quaternion field, and 64 moment magnitudes are quantised to carry the text. Latent diffusion: the 8 × 8 DCT coefficients are the latent; noise is added, then guided DDIM steps denoise it towards the watermark target. The denoiser is analytic, not a trained network. DCT micro-marks tile the text and time over every block, so crops keep it.

Limits

Rotation, perspective and photographing a screen will usually destroy the watermark. The payload is not encrypted or signed: anyone can extract it with this page, and anyone can overwrite it by re-embedding. Treat it as a marker, not as proof.
